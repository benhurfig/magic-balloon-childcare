CHILD CARE IN FRAMINGHAM

Full-Time Child Care for the Early Years

Magic Balloon Childcare provides Full-Time family child care in Framingham for children from 3 months to 3 years old, with a warm environment built around care, play, communication and everyday discovery.

Ages 3 Months–3 Years • Monday–Friday • Full-Time • Vouchers Accepted

CARE THAT GROWS WITH THEM

For Children From 3 Months to 3 Years

The first years of life bring rapid growth, new skills and constant discovery. Our child care environment is designed around the needs of young children during these important early stages.

Early care, comfort and age-appropriate experiences.

EVERYDAY LEARNING

Learning Happens Throughout the Day

Young children learn through everyday experiences. Play, conversations, movement, stories and hands-on activities create opportunities to explore, participate and build new skills.

P

Learning Through Play

Open-ended play helps children make choices, try ideas and discover how things work.

C

Hands-on materials invite imagination, participation and new ways to express ideas.

Conversations, songs and stories make communication a natural part of the day.

M

Age-appropriate movement gives children opportunities to explore and build confidence.
