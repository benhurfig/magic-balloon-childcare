ABOUT MAGIC BALLOON

Meet Lucineia Fernandes

Behind Magic Balloon Childcare is a provider who believes that young children need more than supervision — they need care, attention, opportunities to learn and a place where they can feel comfortable being themselves.

MY BACKGROUND

Education That Supports Everyday Care

Lucineia studied Pedagogy in Brazil with a focus on Early Childhood Education and later completed postgraduate studies in Early Childhood Education.

Her educational background helps shape the way activities, communication and everyday experiences are approached at Magic Balloon Childcare.

MY APPROACH TO CARE

Care That Respects Each Child's Early Years

The early years are full of discovery. At Magic Balloon Childcare, everyday care is built around creating opportunities for children to play, communicate, explore and participate in age-appropriate experiences.

The goal is to provide a warm environment where children receive attentive care while building confidence and experiencing the world around them.

MAGIC BALLOON TODAY

A Family Child Care in Framingham
