HOW CHILDREN LEARN

Learning Through Play, Creativity and Discovery

At Magic Balloon Childcare, children learn through play, hands-on activities, movement and meaningful everyday experiences. Our full-time family child care environment in Framingham gives children opportunities to explore, communicate, create and grow throughout the day.

Learning Through Play

Play gives children opportunities to explore ideas, make choices and learn naturally through everyday experiences.

Creative Activities

Art and hands-on activities encourage imagination, curiosity and opportunities for children to express themselves.

Language & Communication

Stories, songs and daily conversations create opportunities to develop communication and language skills.

Movement & Exploration

Movement and age-appropriate exploration help children stay active, discover their surroundings and build confidence.
