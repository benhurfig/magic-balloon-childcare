Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Magic Balloon Childcare handles information connected with this website and the Family Request process.

Effective date: August 27, 2026

1. Information we receive

We may receive information that you choose to provide when you contact us or submit a Family Request, including:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, and preferred way to be contacted.
  • Basic information about your child that the form asks you to provide.
  • Your desired start date, schedule, type of care, and other child care needs.
  • Messages or questions you send to us.

Please do not send medical or other highly sensitive information unless we specifically request it through an appropriate process.

2. Technical website information

When you visit the website, our hosting and security providers may automatically process basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, requested pages, date and time, and security or error logs. Cookie consent preferences may be stored in your browser so the website can remember your choice.

3. How we use information

We use information to review Family Requests, respond to questions, communicate about care needs, availability, visits, and possible next steps, operate and secure the website, and comply with reasonable legal or recordkeeping obligations. We may contact you by email, phone, or text when you request contact or provide those details for follow-up.

4. Technology providers

We use service providers to operate this website and receive requests. These may include Cloudflare for website hosting and security and SmartMate for the Family Request experience. Phone, SMS, email, and linked social or business-profile services are operated by their respective providers. Those providers may process information under their own privacy terms.

5. Google Analytics and your choice

We use Google Analytics to understand how this website is used. Analytics is denied by default and is activated only after you choose to accept analytics cookies. After consent, Google Analytics may measure page views and website interactions. You may decline analytics or change your choice later through Cookie Settings in the footer.

6. Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with technology providers that help us operate the website or process a request, when reasonably necessary to protect the website or people, or when required by law.

7. Retention and security

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed to respond to your request, manage related communications, meet recordkeeping needs, or protect our services. We use reasonable safeguards, but no website, transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your choices and requests

You may ask us to review, correct, or delete information you submitted, subject to information we may reasonably need to retain. You can also change the website's cookie choice through Cookie Settings in the footer. To make a privacy request, contact us using the details below.

9. Third-party links

This website links to services that we do not control, including the Family Request service, Google Business Profile, Instagram, email, telephone, and SMS services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

10. Policy updates

We may update this policy when our website, practices, or service providers change. The effective date above will identify the current version.
